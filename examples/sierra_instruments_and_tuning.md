# The Sierra Story (1991 to 2025 and beyond): instruments and tuning

**What it is:** a brand-film soundtrack with three layers: a male narrator, sound design (nature and vehicle sounds) and a cinematic music score.

## Tuning
| | Result | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Reference pitch | **A4 = 440 Hz (standard concert tuning)**, not 432 | High: guitar −1 cent, strings 0, piano +4 |
| Key | **D minor** (main chords D5/Dm, B♭, C(sus4), with Dsus2/Dsus4 colours) | Medium-high |
| Guitar tuning | **Looks like standard tuning** (lowest notes E2–G2). Little sign of drop-D, even though the song is in D. | Medium |
| Tempo | **137 BPM**; the big hits feel like half-time (~68 BPM) | Medium |

## Instruments
1. **Sub bass / 808-style synth bass**: deep, clean, almost a pure sine wave, mono. 60% of its energy is below 60 Hz. The model hears "rumble / throbbing", not a bass guitar. Root notes D, B♭, F.
2. **Strings (cinematic string section)**: violin and bowed-string ensemble, warm, sustained, very wide stereo. Most likely an orchestral sample library. Plays around D, A, G, F.
3. **Guitar**: clean plucked or picked guitar, rhythmic, wide, low in the mix (−17 dB). Comes in around 0:25–0:28 and plays through most of the film.
4. **Cinematic percussion and impacts**: trailer-style booms and hits rather than a pop drum kit. Big hits at 1:10, 2:05, 2:20 and 2:34.
5. **Soft piano or keys** (faint): mainly in the quiet section around 1:30–1:55.
6. **Voice**: male narrator throughout. There is no singing.
7. **Sound design**: birds (0:00), rain and thunder (0:20), horse hooves (0:30), vehicle and engine sounds, water and waves (1:40, 2:20), camera shutter (2:50).

## Shape of the music
- 0:00–0:30 Intro: narration over sparse music and nature sounds
- 0:30–1:01 Build: guitar and strings come in, percussion grows
- **1:10 First peak / big hit**
- 1:33–1:55 Breakdown: energy drops, soft keys
- 2:05 Rebuild → **2:34–2:44 Climax**
- 2:50 End: narration and camera shutter
